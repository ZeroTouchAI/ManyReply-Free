# Music for the Reels

Every `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav` or `.ogg` in this folder is used as background music for the daily Reel.
The tracks take turns, one per post. Each is faded in, faded out and trimmed to the length of the Reel.

Only put tracks here that may be used commercially: public domain / CC0, a royalty-free licence you hold,
or music you made. Instagram can mute or remove a Reel that uses copyrighted music.

With no tracks here, Reels go out silent.

## Tracks in this folder

All were released by their authors under CC0 (public domain dedication) on OpenGameArt. The slow lo-fi set from 3 October 2026 was swapped on 1 November 2026 for upbeat tracks (funk, house, electro), the owner's choice: energy keeps people watching.
The longer ones are trimmed to their first minute: a Reel uses about 25 seconds.
No credit is required; the sources are kept here so the licence can be checked.
