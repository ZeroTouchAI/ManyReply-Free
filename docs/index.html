Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The software is free and open source (MIT). Zernio, the service that connects to Instagram, is separate and has its own pricing; its first two connected accounts are free.

Where does my API key go?

It stays in your browser (this tab, unless you tick “keep me connected on this device”) and is sent only to zernio.com. There is no server of ours in the middle, and the page is built so it can’t talk to anyone else.

Do I have to ask people to follow first?

No. It’s one switch. Leave it on and non-followers are asked to follow before they get the link; turn it off and everyone gets the link right away.

What if I don’t have a Zernio account?

Create one at zernio.com, connect your Instagram business or creator account, and make an API key. The dashboard walks you through it.

Does it work on Reels?

You can paste a Reel link or pick it from your recent posts. We haven’t tested Reels yet, so try one first; “Any post” is the fallback.

Is it affiliated with Instagram or Meta?

No. ManyReply DM is an independent tool built on Zernio. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Instagram, Meta or Zernio.
